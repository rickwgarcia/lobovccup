Team 11: SMART Curbs
Pathway (Startup or VC): Startup
If Startup - What's your idea?: We’re building SMART Curbs, a new way for streets to communicate with the people using them. By embedding sensors and LED lighting into  sidewalk curbs, the system can respond in real time to pedestrians, cyclists, and vehicles. The curb can light up to guide crossings, warn drivers, or highlight hazards, making streets safer and easier to navigate. Instead of static infrastructure, SMART Curbs turn the edge of the street into an interactive safety network that helps cities create smarter, more connected transportation systems.
